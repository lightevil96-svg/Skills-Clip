# Reference style protocol

## Three independent layers
1. Universal principles: source integrity, context, legibility, causal order,
   attribution and source-free ZIP delivery.
2. User style profile: observed/preferred typography, palette, placement, camera
   language, zoom behavior, transitions, SFX, meme placement, motion graphics,
   pacing and overall identity. Apply only to its declared user/content scope.
3. Project instructions: current brief, output constraints, exceptions and
   approved assets. Document conflicts and the chosen resolution.

A project preference may override a style preference, but cannot manufacture
source facts. Conversation speaker-follow rules remain the default. A user
request for a different composition must be explicit and recorded.

## Extraction procedure
- Inspect representative setup, payoff, quiet and expressive portions of each
  supplied reference. Record source ID, time range, method and confidence.
- Measure distributions/ranges where useful. Do not prescribe a cut every
  two seconds because three shots happen to have that length.
- Describe font characteristics if the exact family is unknown. Mark inferred
  colors approximate when compression or grading prevents precise sampling.
- Separate what you saw from what you want to adapt. An absent SFX in inspected
  frames is UNKNOWN without audio review, not proof of silence.
- Record each trait's applicability, adaptation and exceptions in
  [style-profile schema](../../schemas/style-profile.schema.json).
- Reproduce function with original, licensed or user-cleared assets. Do not
  extract copyrighted logos, stickers, music or fonts for redistribution.
- Preview the profile on the current content. Keep the source's timing and
  emotion; reduce decorative traits when they obscure the new material.

The historical [conversation reference](../../vtuber-conversation-clip/references/style-reference.md)
describes one sample. Its palette, 1–3 second visual beats and gag timestamps
are observations, not global defaults. Its footage is absent from this repo;
do not claim to have re-inspected that video from reading the document.

## Output
Keep `style-profile.json` separate, then reference its ID and the applied or
rejected traits in the edit plan. Use UNKNOWN when a trait cannot be observed.
Include project overrides and the evidence for each accepted treatment.
